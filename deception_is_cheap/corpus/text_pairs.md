# Text Pairs — Deception is Cheap Corpus

Three pairs of investor communications. All fraudulent texts are
verbatim quotes from federal complaints or enforcement actions. All
compliant texts are filings from regulated entities with no enforcement
history. All labels come from federal legal proceedings — nothing was
labeled by the authors.
